This Cursed Throne

A political TTRPG of dynasty and decline. Each player leads a noble House across decades, not single sessions — characters age, die, and are succeeded, while the realm itself edges toward a Calamity only the whole table can survive together.

Status

The game is fully playtestable end to end. Setup, the decade loop, every task and check, births/deaths, succession, the throne, and two complete Calamity modules (Magic-Born and External Invasion) are locked. This site is the current source of truth for running a session — start with How to Start.

What you need at the table

No physical components exist yet — printed cards and boards are coming later. Everything below is off-the-shelf and enough to fully playtest the game today.

ItemWhat it's for
Standard playing cards
(52-card decks, no jokers)		 Every table in this game — House Traits, Character Traits, the Events Deck, Birth Deal, death/birth checks, "random house" targeting — is a flip from a standard deck, reshuffled after every draw. A couple of decks let simultaneous draws (e.g. everyone rolling their Leader at once during setup) happen without a bottleneck — shared at the table, borrowed freely.
Some six-sided dice Every Task and Trial is a dice pool of d6s (success on 5–6). Pools scale with your stats but most stay modest — even the Competitive band-width rule treats 14+ dice as its top tier, not the norm. A shared stash at the table covers routine play; borrow freely rather than dividing dice up per player.
Index cards Every decade, each House secretly writes one Scene intent on a card before all cards flip simultaneously (Phase 2). Also handy for house reference notes. Reusable mini whiteboards work just as well.
Some poker chips (a few colors) Track Land, Manpower, and Wealth per House — the stats that change almost every decade. A pile in the middle of the table represents the unclaimed pool that Static actions like Broker Deal and Develop Territory claim from.
A few distinct tokens
(different from the chips above)		 Mark which House currently holds Destiny (at most one per House), and bank Calamity prep tokens — Magic-Born's Wards, Invasion's Bulwarks (max 3 each per House).
Paper or a whiteboard Track Honor/Infamy, Piety, Legend, and Cunning (these change less often than the chip-tracked stats, so plain notes work fine). Also holds the house order ring and the Doom Clock — draw a simple numbered track and move a token along it as it advances.

All of the above are shared table resources, not per-player kits — borrow freely mid-decade.

Tip

You don't need custom trait or event cards to play right now — every table (House Traits, Character Traits, the Events Deck) is a direct suit+rank lookup on the Tables & Decks page. Flip a standard card, look up the result, done.
