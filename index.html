This Cursed Throne

A Game of Thrones–style political TTRPG. Each player leads a noble House across decades, not single sessions — characters age, die, and are succeeded, while the realm itself edges toward a Calamity only the whole table can survive together.

Status

The game is fully playtestable end to end. Setup, the decade loop, every task and check, births/deaths, succession, the throne, and two complete Calamity modules (Magic-Born and External Invasion) are locked. This site is the current source of truth for running a session — start with How to Start.

What you need at the table

No physical components exist yet — printed cards and boards are coming later. Everything below is off-the-shelf and enough to fully playtest the game today.

ItemHow muchWhat it's for
Standard playing cards
(52-card decks, no jokers)		 3–4 decks Every table in this game — House Traits, Character Traits, the Events Deck, Birth Deal, death/birth checks, "random house" targeting — is a flip from a standard deck, reshuffled after every draw. Multiple decks let simultaneous draws (e.g. everyone rolling their Leader at once during setup) happen without a bottleneck.
Six-sided dice (d6) 20–30 per player, plus a shared bucket of 50+ Every Task and Trial is a dice pool of d6s (success on 5–6). Pools scale with your stats and can grow large — combo actions sum two stats, and a Revolt or a Calamity's Final Round can pool multiple houses' dice together. A dice tray or bag helps.
Index cards 100–150 per campaign, or reusable mini whiteboards Every decade, each House secretly writes one Scene intent on a card before all cards flip simultaneously (Phase 2). Also handy for house reference notes.
Poker chips (3–4 colors) ~20–30 per color per player, plus a large shared "unclaimed pool" stash Track Land, Manpower, and Wealth per House — the stats that change almost every decade. A pile in the middle of the table represents the unclaimed pool that Static actions like Broker Deal and Develop Territory claim from.
Distinct tokens (a different color/shape from the above) A handful Mark which House currently holds Destiny (at most one per House), and bank Calamity prep tokens — Magic-Born's Wards, Invasion's Bulwarks (max 3 each per House).
Paper or a whiteboard 1 per House, plus a shared sheet Track Honor/Infamy, Piety, Legend, and Cunning (these change less often than the chip-tracked stats, so plain notes work fine). The shared sheet holds the house order ring and the Doom Clock — draw a simple numbered track and move a token along it as it advances.
Tip

You don't need custom trait or event cards to play right now — every table (House Traits, Character Traits, the Events Deck) is a direct suit+rank or rank-only lookup on the Tables & Decks page. Flip a standard card, look up the result, done.
