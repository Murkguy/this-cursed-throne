Tables & Decks

Every card table in the game, in one place. All use a standard 52-card deck, reshuffled after every draw.

House Trait Table Locked

Every house has 4 traits: 2 chosen freely, 2 rolled (flip 1 card, direct suit+rank lookup — no math). A Magical Order house has 5 (see House Archetypes).

Ace-High — the one deliberate exception

Rank here runs the opposite of everywhere else in the game: 2 is worst, Ace is best. Ranks 2–4 skew weak benefit/real cost (2 also carries a bespoke restriction beyond dice), 5–7 are a net-zero sidegrade, 8–10 skew strong benefit/light cost, and J/Q/K/A abandon dice math entirely for unique mechanics.

Duplicates reroll. Sickly and Long-Lived are mutually exclusive — direct contradictions, reroll if you already hold the other.

Events Deck Locked

Drawn every decade at Kingdom Scene. Suit = category, rank = quality (Ace-low, standard convention). Deck reshuffles after every draw.

How a card resolves

The houses are a closed system — they only otherwise act on each other through Tasks and Scenes. The events deck is everything outside that system: weather, disasters, foreign courts, traveling spectacle, omens.

ShapeWhat it means
POOLAdds to (or on low ranks, removes from) the unclaimed pool. No targeting.
RANKEDTargets a house via its standing in one stat: ♥Honor/Infamy, ♦Wealth, ♣Legend, ♠Piety. Low rank hits the current highest (fall from grace); high rank helps the current lowest (ladder up).
RANDOMFlip 1 additional card; its rank, counted around the ring from the King's seat, picks the house.
ALLApplies to every house equally.
CONTESTAny house may declare interest; each flips 1 card, highest wins (redraw ties).

PERSONAL cards (plagues, accidents, formative journeys) hit a person instead of a stat — a death check or gains 1 new trait, always the fixed House Leader or current heir, never a player choice.

POOL amounts are randomized — flip 1 card. Additions: 2-4→+1, 5-7→+2, 8-10→+3, J-K-A→+4. Removals: 2-6→-1, 7-10→-2, J-K-A→-3.

Calamity Modules

One module is chosen at setup — the shared threat the whole campaign builds toward. All four share the same endgame shape; each defines its own theme, Doom Clock trigger, prep action, and Final Round trials.

Shared Endgame Shape

The Final Round triggers the decade a module's Doom Clock hits max — the last decade of the campaign. Every house still standing faces 3 sequential trials, each COMPETITIVE against the Calamity itself. The Calamity's pool for Trial N = (current decade number) × N — a Final Round in decade 10 pits Trial 1 against a pool of 10, Trial 2 against 20, Trial 3 against 30.

Houses may ally on any trial, pooling dice into one combined roll — the outcome applies to everyone allied, but consequences still land individually. Every death check rolled is one age-tier worse than normal. Ruin/Extinction knocks a house out of contention immediately.

OutcomeWhat happens
Decisive winLose 1 point from ONE of the trial's two pool stats.
Narrow winLose 1 point from EACH of the trial's two pool stats, and the player's choice of ONE relative takes a death check.
LossLose 5 points from EVERY one of the house's 7 stats (floored at 0), and EVERY relative tracked by the house takes a death check.

The Loss penalty stacks across trials (-10 for two lost, -15 for three) — for most houses, even one lost trial threatens Ruin, and losing any two guarantees it. Determining the winner: among houses still standing after all 3 trials, sum all 7 stats (Honor/Infamy as absolute magnitude) — highest total wins the campaign.

Every module also grants exactly one dedicated prep action — a Task slot spent today for an edge at the Final Round.

Magic-Born Locked

Magic itself is the threat — the more legendary the realm's houses become, the closer that mark pulls reality toward rupture.

Doom Clock: 0–10. Advances when the King/Legend curse draw's kept card is Ace/2/3.

Prep action — Ritual of the Unbroken Ward: Magic, Static, Legend+Cunning, once/decade. Success banks 1 Ward (max 3). Failure advances the Doom Clock by 1. Spend Wards during the Final Round to upgrade a trial's outcome one tier each.

A foreign power coming to take the realm entire — indifferent to who holds the throne.
