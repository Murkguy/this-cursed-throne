# Character Trait Table

**STATUS: DRAFTED**, first pass, awaiting review.

The 13-entry mixed (boon/burden) trait table used by Birth Deal (see
`setup-order.md` step 4 and every later birth via `birth-death.md`).
Separate pool from the house trait table (`tables/house-traits.md`) —
a Cruel house can still raise a Doting relative. House traits describe
what a whole dynasty shares across generations; these describe one
person's temperament.

**Rolling:** each trait slot is filled by flipping 1 card — only the
**rank** matters here (13 rows, Ace through King), suit is irrelevant
and ignored. **Ace-LOW** — same convention as Birth Deal's trait-count
roll and the events deck (Ace is the weakest rank, King the
strongest), the reverse of the house trait table's deliberate Ace-high
exception.

**Duplicates:** if a roll produces a trait the character already has,
reroll.

**Magic conversion** is handled at the Birth Deal level, not by a row
in this table — an Ace of Spades is always eligible for conversion,
any face card is also eligible if the house has the Magical trait (see
`setup-order.md` step 4.3). No card here needs its own Magic-specific
entry.

Every trait cuts both ways, same principle as house traits — no pure
upside. Low ranks skew weak benefit / real cost, high ranks skew
strong benefit / light cost — same low=bad/high=good curve used
everywhere else in the game.

| Rank | Trait | Benefit | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Cowardly** | +1 die on Espionage tasks — quick to vanish, quicker to notice danger. | -2 dice on War tasks — the first to break and run. |
| 2 | **Timid** | +1 die on Stewardship tasks — careful, risk-averse management. | -2 dice on War tasks — flinches from confrontation. |
| 3 | **Vengeful** | +1 die on Espionage tasks — holds a grudge long enough to act on it. | -2 dice on Diplomacy tasks — can't let old wounds go in negotiation. |
| 4 | **Melancholic** | +1 die on Magic tasks — drawn inward, and to what's stranger than the world admits. | -2 dice on Trade tasks — no taste for haggling or profit. |
| 5 | **Doting** | +2 dice on Diplomacy tasks — warmth wins allies. | -2 dice on Espionage tasks — too open for secrets. |
| 6 | **Humble** | +2 dice on Stewardship tasks. | -2 dice on Diplomacy tasks — doesn't play the part courts expect. |
| 7 | **Calculating** | +2 dice on Trade tasks — shrewd, methodical, always counting. | -2 dice on Magic tasks — too grounded in what can be counted to trust in anything that can't. |
| 8 | **Restless** | +2 dice on War tasks — always looking for the next fight, not the next deal. | -2 dice on Trade tasks. |
| 9 | **Hot-Blooded** | +2 dice on War tasks. | -2 dice on Stewardship tasks — no patience for slow administration. |
| 10 | **Prideful** | +2 dice on Diplomacy tasks. | -1 die on Stewardship tasks — too proud to stoop to slow, unglamorous management. |
| J | **Charming** | +2 dice on Diplomacy tasks — effortlessly liked. | -1 die on Espionage tasks — too well-known to move unseen. |
| Q | **Stoic** | +2 dice on Stewardship tasks — unshaken, methodical, hard to rattle. | -1 die on Magic tasks — too practical to put stock in anything beyond what can be seen and touched. |
| K | **Reckless** | +3 dice on War tasks — bold past the point of sense. | -1 die on Espionage tasks — leaves a trail, doesn't care to hide it. |
