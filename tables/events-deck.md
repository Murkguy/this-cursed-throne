# The Events Deck

**STATUS: LOCKED.** All 52 entries confirmed. The table drawn at every
Kingdom Scene (`turn-order.md` Phase 4). Suit = category (flavor),
rank = quality (Ace-low: 2 worst, Ace best — standard convention, NOT
the house trait table's deliberate Ace-high exception). Deck is always
reshuffled after every draw — no cross-event scarcity tracking.

## How a card resolves
The houses are a closed system — they only otherwise act on each
other through Tasks and Scenes. **The events deck is everything
outside that system**: weather, disasters, foreign courts, traveling
spectacle, omens, the wider world the realm sits inside. Every entry
falls into one of five shapes:

- **POOL** — adds to (or on low ranks, removes from) the unclaimed
  pool. No targeting needed.
- **RANKED** — targets a house via its current standing in one stat,
  consistent per suit: **Hearts→Honor/Infamy, Diamonds→Wealth,
  Clubs→Legend, Spades→Piety.** Low ranks hit whichever house
  currently ranks HIGHEST in that stat (a fall from grace); high ranks
  help whichever house currently ranks LOWEST (a ladder up). Ties:
  table's choice among the tied houses.
- **RANDOM** — flip one additional card (not the event card itself);
  its rank, counted around the house ring starting from the King's
  seat (wrapping as needed), picks the house. No King seated: count
  from house-order position 1 instead.
- **ALL** — applies to every house at the table equally.
- **CONTEST** — the wider world offers something worth competing for
  (a tourney, a pageant, a foreign prize). Any house may declare
  interest; each declared house flips 1 card, highest rank wins
  (redraw ties). A house that doesn't declare simply doesn't compete —
  no cost either way.

Magnitude stays small and steady on non-face cards (+1/+2 typical,
-1 on the lowest ranks) so 9 ordinary draws per suit don't flood the
economy over a long campaign — face cards (J/Q/K/A) are where the
bigger, rarer moments live. **Vary WHAT kind of thing is happening,
not just the number attached to it** — a plague, a comet, a foreign
king's visit, and a funeral should all feel like different events even
where the mechanical shape underneath is the same.

**Card format:** each entry below is written the way it'd read on the
physical card — a title, one short line of flavor, and a rule line
terse enough to resolve at a glance without flipping back to this
page.

**PERSONAL effects:** a handful of cards per suit (plagues, accidents,
formative journeys) layer a personal consequence onto their targeting
shape instead of a stat change — either **takes a death check** (same
age/trait-adjusted band as the Fate Check) or **gains 1 new trait**
(flip a card on the character trait table, `tables/character-
traits.md`; reroll duplicates). **No player choice of who** — each
card names a fixed role, always either the **House Leader** or the
**current heir** (per the house's Succession Custom; if no heir
exists yet, the Leader instead). Something happens to the house, not
by it. These aren't a separate shape — they're layered on top of
RANKED, RANDOM, or ALL exactly like a stat change would be, just
hitting a person instead of a house's numbers.

**The effect itself should vary too, not just the target.** A card's
Shape (POOL/RANKED/RANDOM/ALL/CONTEST) says WHO it touches; it doesn't
have to always mean "a stat moves by 1-3." The same toolbox used
elsewhere in the game works here — mix these in freely alongside plain
stat changes:

- **Stat change** — the baseline, still most common, but not the only
  move.
- **PERSONAL** — death check or new trait (see above).
- **Banked bonus** — +2 dice on this house's next attempt at a named
  task this decade (a door opened, not cash in hand).
- **Free attempt** — this house may immediately attempt a named STATIC
  action (e.g. Forge Alliance) at no Task Assignment cost — still
  rolls normally, just doesn't cost the slot.
- **Cursed/blessed luck** — this house's next relevant card draw
  (Birth Deal, another event, etc.) is drawn as 2-and-keep-worst (or
  best) — reuses the same mechanic Magic's Curse/Bless already use.
- **Alliance bond** — grants the same lasting +2-dice-acting-together
  bond Forge Alliance/Arrange Marriage create, between this house and
  one of its choosing — no roll, just narratively earned.

**POOL amounts are randomized, not flat** — the pool is the main way
resources ever enter the game, so a fixed "+2 every time" would make
it feel mechanical instead of eventful. Whenever a POOL card's rule
gives an amount, flip 1 additional card instead of using a set number:

- **Addition cards:** 2-4 → **+1**, 5-7 → **+2**, 8-10 → **+3**,
  J-K-A → **+4**.
- **Removal cards:** 2-6 → **-1**, 7-10 → **-2**, J-K-A → **-3**.

Additions get the wider range (more of the deck's "spice" lives in the
upside) — removals stay mostly small since low-rank cards are already
the rarer, punishing end of the curve.
