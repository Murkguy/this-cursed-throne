# Character Trait Bank

Not the character trait table itself — that's still undrafted (see the
open item in `house-traits.md`). This is a holding pen for traits that
were originally written for the house trait table but turned out to
read as individual personality rather than institutional/dynastic
character, and got pulled out during the Hearts suit review
(2026-08-11).

The rule that sent them here: a house trait should describe something
a whole dynasty shares across generations (a military tradition, a
bloodline curse, a mercantile reputation). A personality quirk belongs
to one person, not an institution — "we won't have a house full of
timid people."

These are preserved as-is (same mechanics, same magnitude) as strong
starting material for the character trait table whenever that gets
drafted. Not guaranteed to keep their exact numbers or rank positions —
the character table will need its own magnitude curve and rank
assignments once its structure is decided.
